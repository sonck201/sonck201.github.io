What I offer

Architecture & Platform Design

A new product, a new regulation, or growth the current system was never built for, and no clear picture of what the target platform should look like.

You get

  • Target architecture with service boundaries and integration contracts
  • Key decisions written down with their trade-offs
  • Diagrams as code the team can keep up to date
  • Phased delivery plan and estimates stakeholders can trust

A focused design phase, then hands-on technical leadership through delivery.

Legacy Modernization & Refactoring

A monolith or a vendor lock-in that slows every change, and a client base that cannot afford a big-bang rewrite.

You get

  • Impact analysis of the existing system
  • Refactoring roadmap with safe, reversible steps
  • Abstraction layers that keep existing clients working
  • Migration executed with the team, behavior preserved

A health check first, then a phased roadmap delivered sprint by sprint.

AI-Driven Engineering

AI coding tools are in the building, but without shared workflows, guardrails, or measurable results they stay individual experiments.

You get

  • Pilot of AI-assisted story drafting, implementation, and review
  • Guardrails that keep engineers accountable for every change
  • Codebase context tooling for large legacy repositories
  • Before/after metrics to decide on the rollout

Pilot with a small group of engineers, then a rollout playbook for the whole team.

How I work

  1. 01

    Discover

    Understand the business goal, the users, and the constraints before touching the code.

  2. 02

    Assess

    Analyze the current system, its risks, and its technical debt, and size the options.

  3. 03

    Design

    Agree on the target architecture and a phased plan, with every trade-off on the table.

  4. 04

    Deliver

    Lead the team through delivery with clear standards, reviews, and early risk mitigation.

  5. 05

    Hand over

    Leave documentation, diagrams, and a team that can run and grow the system on its own.
