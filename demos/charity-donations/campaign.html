Clean water wells for 12 villages in Kasora Valley

📍 Kasora Valley · East region 💧 Water & sanitation
Story Updates 4 Budget breakdown Donors (1,284)

In Kasora Valley, families walk an average of 6 km a day to collect water from open ponds. Children, mostly girls, miss school to make the trip, and waterborne illness peaks every dry season.

Together with local water committees, we will drill 12 solar-powered wells, train 24 caretakers, and fund five years of maintenance, bringing safe water within 500 m of 8,400 people.
